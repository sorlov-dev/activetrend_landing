Welcome to ActiveTrend. ActiveTrend is a product developed and operated by Sergei Orlov (independent entrepreneur / monotributista based in Argentina). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy covers the ActiveTrend website at activetrend.app.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal information is Sergei Orlov.
Location: Buenos Aires, Argentina.
Contact: contact@orlovs.dev

2. Information We Collect

We collect your email address when you voluntarily subscribe to our waitlist. That is the only piece of information this website asks you for. There are no user accounts, no login and no advertising on this website.

When you visit the site, basic technical information (such as browser type and operating system) is processed automatically by the providers that host and deliver it, to keep the website functioning securely. This is described in section 6.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used strictly to notify you about the launch of the ActiveTrend app. We rely on your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for this processing. You give that consent by submitting the waitlist form, and you can withdraw it at any time.

4. How the Waitlist Email Is Processed (Brevo)

When you submit the waitlist form, your email address is sent directly from your browser to Brevo (formerly Sendinblue), at the domain sibforms.com. Brevo acts as our email service provider: it stores your address on our waitlist and will deliver the launch announcement to you.

The only field we send is the email address you typed into the form. We do not send your name, and we do not attach any other information to it. Brevo processes this data as our processor, under its own privacy policy and data protection terms.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

This website sets no cookies of its own. It stores two values in your browser's local storage — theme and lang — which remember whether you chose the light or dark version of the site, and which language you last selected. These values stay in your browser, are never sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's site data. You can also instruct your browser to refuse all cookies.

6. Hosting and Third-Party Services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and served through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which sits in front of it as a proxy. Because every request to activetrend.app passes through Cloudflare, Cloudflare receives your IP address, the time of the request and basic technical details of your browser, and keeps them in its own logs. Cloudflare also runs its own Real User Monitoring (RUM) beacon on this site, which measures page loading performance from your browser.

The website loads its typeface from Google Fonts (Google LLC) and its icons from the cdnjs service (Cloudflare, Inc.). To deliver those files, both providers necessarily receive your IP address and basic technical details of your browser, and may keep them in their own server logs under their own privacy policies. We do not receive those logs and have no access to them.

We do not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any profiling or cross-site tracking service on this website.

7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. (Compliant with CCPA). We only share data with the trusted third-party service providers named in this policy, who adhere to strict data protection standards.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction, or erasure of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten"). You can opt-out of future communications at any time by contacting us at contact@orlovs.dev.

9. The ActiveTrend App

The ActiveTrend Android app is covered by its own App Privacy Policy. It describes what the app stores on your device, and exactly what the optional AI Insight feature sends to Google Vertex AI when you enable it.
